# ISS_tracker
The ISS-tracker indicates—by flashing yellow—when the ISS is at a distance of between 500 km and 1000 km from the location. The LED flashes green when the ISS is less than 500 km away.
When the LED lights up yellow, a tone sounds periodically in sync with the flashing LED; when the LED flashes green, a siren sounds. To amplify the sound, a small audio amplifier and a speaker have been integrated instead of a buzzer. The amplifier draws its supply voltage from the board, and the audio signal is fed from pin D8 via a voltage divider. The amplifier can be switched on and off using a small switch.

This project is based on an idea by Arpan Mondal; see https://www.instructables.com/ISS-Antenna-Indicates-When-the-ISS-Is-Overhead. You can find the assembly instructions and the 3D printing files for the individual parts there.
